# Message to send to a streamer (copy, edit, send)

Hi {name}! I'm {your name}, I run a small YouTube Shorts channel, **{channel name}**, that shows one
cool {game} feature per Short (a few seconds of gameplay + a voice-over explaining the feature).

Could I use short clips (15-40 s) from your {game} streams / VODs?

- Only the gameplay is used. Your face, voice, chat and overlays are removed.
- Every Short credits you in the description with a link to your channel.
- I will not use any story spoilers, glitches/exploits, or anything you tell me to skip.
- If you ever want a clip removed, tell me and it is deleted the same day.

If that's OK, a simple "yes, you can use my gameplay" reply here is enough for me.
Thanks a lot, and enjoy the launch!

---

## After they say yes
1. Take a screenshot of the reply and save it in `proofs/` (this folder is git-ignored).
2. In `sources.yaml` set `permission: granted` and write where the proof is in `permission_proof`.
3. Keep a note of any restrictions they gave you (e.g. "no clips with my donations on screen").

## Who to ask
* Smaller and mid-size creators (they usually reply and often like the free credit/exposure).
* Creators who already say in their channel/stream description that clipping is welcome.
* YouTube videos that carry a Creative Commons licence still show Rockstar's game footage,
  so a CC tag from the streamer does **not** replace thinking about the game publisher's policy.
